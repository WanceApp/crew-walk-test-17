# AI code-review pricing for small teams

*Brief, Nov 2026. Figures come from search snippets and vendor-pricing aggregators, not from vendor pages opened directly. Verify before buying.*

## Summary
Most AI code-review tools charge per developer seat, roughly $12-$48 per month, with a free tier for public or open-source repos. A few (Greptile, Qodo) add usage credits on top of the seat. For a 5-person team, expect about $120-$150 per month at mainstream tiers (5 x $24-$30).

## Market size
- 2025 global AI code review market: about $1.4B (Market Intelo, estimate), about $1.65B (Trendx Insights, estimate, broader scope). One report's regional figures sum to about $0.9B (pmarketresearch, estimate).
- Forecasts: $4.9B by 2032 (pmarketresearch) to about $10B by 2034 (Market Intelo, Trendx). All are analyst estimates, and definitions differ.

## Key players (list prices, 2026, as reported)
- **CodeRabbit** - Free (public repos), Lite $12, Pro $24/dev/mo annual ($30 monthly), Pro Plus $48.
- **Greptile** - $30/seat/mo with 50 review credits included, extra credits $1 each.
- **Qodo** - Free tier; Teams about $30/user/mo (sources also report $19 and $38).
- **GitHub Copilot** - $19 Individual / $39 Business per seat; from 1 Jun 2026 code review also uses AI credits ($0.01 each) and Actions minutes.
- **Graphite** - Starter $20/user/mo, includes its Diamond AI review.

## Trends
- Per-seat pricing is the default; credit/usage billing is spreading (Greptile, Qodo, Copilot's June 2026 change).
- Annual billing discounts (about 20% at CodeRabbit) and free tiers for open source are the main small-team levers.
- Review is bundled into broader platforms (Copilot, Graphite), which blurs standalone price comparisons.

## Risks and unknowns
- Aggregators disagree (e.g. CodeRabbit $15 vs $24; Qodo $19/$30/$38); vendor pages were not checked directly.
- Not established: real cost under credit billing at a given PR volume, seat minimums, enterprise or discount terms, and any price change after Nov 2026.
- Market-size reports are paywalled; only snippets were read.
